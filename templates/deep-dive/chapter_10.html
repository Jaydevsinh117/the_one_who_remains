Chapter 10: Vibhuti Yoga

Learn how the Divine manifests in the world — in all that is powerful, beautiful, wise, and inspiring.

🧘 Overview

Krishna reveals His divine opulences — showing how every greatness in the universe is but a spark of His infinite glory. By meditating on these manifestations, devotees can fix their minds on the Supreme.

📜 Key Teachings

  • All divine qualities and glories in the universe are reflections of Krishna’s power.
  • Worship can begin by recognizing the divine in the extraordinary aspects of creation.
  • Knowledge of Krishna’s vibhutis leads to devotion with awe and wonder.

🔖 Selected Verse

“Know that all opulent, beautiful and glorious creations spring from but a spark of My splendor.”
– Bhagavad Gita 10.41

🌱 Practical Reflection

When you see a mountain’s majesty, a teacher’s wisdom, a warrior’s courage, or a poet’s beauty — see Krishna. Let this vision of divine presence in all excellence draw your heart deeper into devotion.
