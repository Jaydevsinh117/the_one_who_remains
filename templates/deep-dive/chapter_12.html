Chapter 12: Bhakti Yoga

The path of devotion — Krishna describes the qualities of His dearest devotees and the essence of pure love.

🧘 Overview

Krishna explains that the path of Bhakti (devotion) is the most accessible and cherished by the Divine. Devotees who are humble, steady, free from malice, and who love with unwavering faith are especially dear to Him.

📜 Key Teachings

  • Devotion is higher than knowledge and works when done with full surrender.
  • True devotees are free from ego, envy, anger, and are calm in joy and sorrow alike.
  • Bhakti brings closeness to the Divine — it is simple, pure, and heartfelt.

🔖 Selected Verse

“He who has no ill will to any being, who is friendly and compassionate, free from possessiveness and ego, equal in pleasure and pain, and forgiving — he is dear to Me.”
– Bhagavad Gita 12.13

🌱 Practical Reflection

Bhakti invites us to live with an open heart. In moments of gratitude, prayer, or loving service, feel your connection with the Divine. Even small acts done with devotion carry immense spiritual power.
