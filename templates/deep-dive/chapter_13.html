Chapter 13: Kshetra Kshetrajna Vibhaga Yoga

The distinction between the body (Kshetra) and the soul (Kshetrajna) — the field of action and the knower within.

🧘 Overview

Krishna explains the fundamental difference between the perishable body and the eternal soul. The body is the field of action, and the Self is the knower of that field. By understanding this distinction, one gains true wisdom.

📜 Key Teachings

  • The body is the field (kshetra), where experiences arise and karma plays out.
  • The soul (kshetrajna) is the eternal witness — the conscious being within the body.
  • True knowledge lies in understanding both the field and its knower, and recognizing the presence of the Supreme within all beings.

🔖 Selected Verse

“He who sees the Supreme Lord existing equally in all beings, the imperishable within the perishable — truly sees.”
– Bhagavad Gita 13.27

🌱 Practical Reflection

Reflect on your inner self as the silent witness of your actions, thoughts, and emotions. See the same divine presence in others — this vision brings humility, peace, and unity.
