Chapter 14: Gunatraya Vibhaga Yoga

The Yoga of the Division of the Three Gunas — Sattva, Rajas, and Tamas — the qualities of nature influencing all beings.

🧘 Overview

Lord Krishna describes how every aspect of life is governed by three qualities — Sattva (purity), Rajas (activity), and Tamas (inertia). Understanding these helps transcend them and attain true freedom.

📜 Key Teachings

  • Sattva brings clarity, wisdom, and harmony.
  • Rajas is rooted in desire, attachment, and restless activity.
  • Tamas leads to ignorance, laziness, and delusion.
  • One must recognize these forces and transcend them to reach the Self beyond nature.

🔖 Selected Verse

“He who transcends the three gunas, who is free from birth, death, and suffering — attains immortality.”
– Bhagavad Gita 14.20

🌱 Practical Reflection

Observe how the three gunas influence your moods, thoughts, and decisions. Cultivate sattva through meditation, service, and sacred knowledge — and move beyond duality to embrace the soul's pure essence.
