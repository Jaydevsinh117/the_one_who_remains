Chapter 16: Daivasura Sampad Vibhaga Yoga

Exploring the traits of the divine and the demoniac — how our nature influences our spiritual path.

🧘 Overview

Lord Krishna classifies human nature into two fundamental types: the divine (daivi) and the demoniac (asuri). The divine qualities lead to liberation, while the demoniac tendencies result in bondage and suffering.

📜 Key Teachings

  • Divine traits include fearlessness, purity, compassion, self-discipline, and wisdom.
  • Demoniac qualities include arrogance, hypocrisy, harshness, and ignorance.
  • Our nature is shaped by our tendencies and choices, not just birth.

🔖 Selected Verse

“Divine qualities lead to liberation, whereas demoniac qualities lead to bondage.”
– Bhagavad Gita 16.5

🌱 Practical Reflection

Cultivate divine virtues in daily life — truthfulness, humility, compassion — and actively root out egoistic impulses. This conscious refinement of character aligns us with our higher nature and the path of liberation.
