Chapter 4: Jnana Yoga

The yoga of knowledge — exploring divine incarnations, wisdom through action, and the illuminating power of truth.

🧘 Overview

In this chapter, Krishna reveals His divine nature and explains how true wisdom liberates the soul. Knowledge is gained through disciplined action and devotion, not just reading or ritual.

📜 Key Teachings

  • Krishna descends in every age to protect righteousness.
  • Action done in knowledge is superior to mechanical rituals.
  • True knowledge burns past karma and leads to liberation.

🔖 Selected Verse

“Whenever righteousness declines and unrighteousness rises, I manifest Myself.”
– Bhagavad Gita 4.7

🌱 Practical Reflection

Seek knowledge not just intellectually but through surrender, devotion, and action. Recognize the divine presence guiding your journey.
