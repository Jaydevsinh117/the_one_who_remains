Chapter 5: Karma Vairagya Yoga

Detachment in action — balancing renunciation and disciplined selfless work.

🧘 Overview

This chapter reconciles renunciation (sannyasa) and selfless action (karma yoga), showing both can lead to liberation when done in the right spirit.

📜 Key Teachings

  • True renunciation is not inaction but detachment from outcomes.
  • The peaceful sage sees all beings as equal.
  • Freedom lies in the inner detachment, not outer withdrawal.

🔖 Selected Verse

“One who neither hates nor desires and is beyond dualities is easily freed from bondage.”
– Bhagavad Gita 5.3

🌱 Practical Reflection

Live in the world without being of the world. Let your heart be quiet amidst the noise of action.
