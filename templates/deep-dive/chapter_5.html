Chapter 5: Karma Vairagya Yoga

The yoga of action and renunciation — choosing the inner detachment of a true yogi while fulfilling one's worldly role.

🧘 Overview

This chapter resolves the apparent conflict between action and renunciation. True renunciation means acting without attachment. Both paths lead to liberation when practiced with discipline and detachment.

📜 Key Teachings

  • The renounced yogi and the karma yogi reach the same spiritual goal.
  • One who sees all beings with equality becomes liberated.
  • Detachment leads to inner joy and bliss in the Self.

🔖 Selected Verse

“He who is free from attachment and liberated in mind, performing actions with self-control, is not tainted by sin.”
– Bhagavad Gita 5.7

🌱 Practical Reflection

Whether you act or renounce, do it in surrender to the Divine. Peace comes not from outer stillness but from inner clarity.
