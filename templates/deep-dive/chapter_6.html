Chapter 6: Dhyana Yoga

The yoga of meditation — mastering the mind and merging in divine consciousness.

🧘 Overview

Dhyana Yoga teaches deep inner meditation and mind control. Krishna shows Arjuna the path to inner peace through disciplined stillness and detachment from the senses.

📜 Key Teachings

  • A true yogi is balanced in success and failure, gain and loss.
  • Meditation should be regular, disciplined, and steady.
  • The yogi sees the Divine in all beings and all beings in the Divine.

🔖 Selected Verse

“A yogi is greater than the ascetic, greater than the man of knowledge, and greater than the man of action.”
– Bhagavad Gita 6.46

🌱 Practical Reflection

True meditation is a lifestyle of stillness, compassion, and balance. Begin with silence — and let it lead you to the Divine.
