Chapter 7: Jnana Vijnana Yoga

The yoga of knowledge and realization — knowing Krishna in full, both materially and spiritually.

🧘 Overview

In this chapter, Krishna reveals His supreme nature as the source of both the physical and metaphysical realms. He describes how few truly know Him and emphasizes devotion as a means to understanding the ultimate reality.

📜 Key Teachings

  • Krishna is the origin of earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intelligence, and ego.
  • Out of thousands, few strive for perfection; of those, few truly know Him.
  • Devotional service is the most effective path to know the Lord.

🔖 Selected Verse

“Among thousands of men, one may strive for perfection, and among those who have achieved perfection, hardly one knows Me in truth.”
– Bhagavad Gita 7.3

🌱 Practical Reflection

Seek not just to read or hear of the Divine, but to *realize* it within. Move from knowing about Krishna to knowing Krishna. Wisdom becomes transformation when paired with devotion.
