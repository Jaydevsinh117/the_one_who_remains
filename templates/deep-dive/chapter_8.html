Chapter 8: Akshara Brahma Yoga

Krishna reveals the secrets of the eternal soul, the nature of time, and the path beyond death.

🧘 Overview

In response to Arjuna's questions, Krishna speaks of Brahman (the imperishable), karma, and remembrance. This chapter teaches that one's consciousness at the time of death determines the soul’s next destination — and that meditating on the Lord leads to liberation.

📜 Key Teachings

  • The soul is eternal and transcendental — it never perishes.
  • Remembering Krishna at the time of death leads to liberation.
  • The wise know the two paths after death: one of light (no return), and one of darkness (rebirth).
  • Meditation and devotion are key to attaining the Supreme.

🔖 Selected Verse

“Whatever state of being one remembers when he quits his body, that state he will attain without fail.”
– Bhagavad Gita 8.6

🌱 Practical Reflection

Prepare your mind each day through spiritual practice. In cultivating remembrance of the Divine, the moment of death becomes a gateway to eternal peace, not fear.
