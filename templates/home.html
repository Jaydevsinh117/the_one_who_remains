About the Project

The Maha Vishnu Project is a sacred initiative to preserve, present, and promote the divine teachings of the Gita, Vishnu Purana, and related scriptures in an immersive, reader-friendly format — accessible to all.

What is the Maha Vishnu Project?

A humble attempt to illuminate the spiritual path by presenting sacred scriptures in an immersive, beautifully designed digital format — enabling seekers, students, and devotees to explore, learn, and connect with divine truths.
